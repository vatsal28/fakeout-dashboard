Current State Scorecard

A practical read on product health.

AreaStatusCall
AcquisitionOkayAdding devices but not fast enough to offset weak retention.
RetentionWeakDAU/MAU and MAU trend point to low repeat habit.
Core utilityStrongAI/LLM checks are heavily used.
MonetisationVery weakPaywall views don't translate to purchases.
Market focusNeeds focusIndia + US should be prioritised before broad expansion.
High impact

Turn FakeOut into a daily trust feed

Users don't naturally fact-check every day. Add a lightweight "Today's viral claims" feed, country filter, and push notifications — giving users a reason to return without needing to bring their own claim.

High impact

Fix the paywall value proposition

593 paywall views and only 3 purchases means the offer isn't compelling. Test clearer bundles: unlimited AI checks, video detection, faster processing, saved history, and source reports.

Medium impact

Protect LLM cost before scaling

LLM calls dominate usage. Add caching, duplicate claim detection, rate limits, and cheaper model routing for low-risk queries. Monetisation should be tied to expensive features.

Medium impact

Localise India growth loops

Create India-specific viral claim coverage, WhatsApp-friendly share flows, Hindi/Hinglish summaries, and SEO landing pages for common scams and viral rumours.

Risk

Don't over-index on ads

With low DAU and short sessions, banner ads will stay weak. Use ads as secondary revenue, not the main business model.

Next experiment

Run a 14-day retention sprint

Pick one metric: Week-1 return rate or DAU/MAU. Ship one daily content loop, one push notification loop, and one smoother "share to FakeOut" flow. Measure before adding more features.
